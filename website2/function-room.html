Worth knowing before you book

The Silcock Suite is upstairs, and there are no toilet facilities on the upper floor — worth bearing in mind if any of your guests will need step-free access nearby.

To check availability and talk through your event, ask for Events Manager Sandra Fairhurst — either by phone or by calling in during opening hours.
