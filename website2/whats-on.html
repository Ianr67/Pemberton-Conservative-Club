What's on

Something on most weeks

Quiz nights, games evenings and cabaret — all for members and members' guests. Dates below are the latest we have; always worth checking at the club too, as things can move.

Fortnightly

Quiz Nights

£1 per person

Doors open 7:30pm, quiz starts 8:15pm. Teams of up to four players. Fancy a flutter alongside the quiz? Play Your Cards Right runs alongside for another £1 a head, with cash prizes up for grabs.

Every other Monday

Darts, Dominoes & Bingo

£5 · £3 no bingo

An evening of darts, dominoes, cards and bingo for members and their guests, running throughout the year. Doors 7:30pm, activities get going from 8:15pm. £3 entry if you'd rather skip the bingo.

Live entertainment for members and members' guests. Tickets are sold at the club during opening hours — they tend to go, so it's worth calling in early. Our 2026 line-up so far:
