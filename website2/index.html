Est. in the heart of Pemberton

A warm welcome, right on Park Road.

Pemberton Conservative Club is a friendly members' social club in the heart of Pemberton, Wigan — just off Junction 26 of the M6, with parking next to the club. Drop in for a quiet drink, a game of snooker, a Wednesday quiz, or book the Silcock Suite for your next celebration.
